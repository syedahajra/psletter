Overall, my teaching statement revolves around creating an engaging and inclusive learning environment for my students. I believe that teaching goes beyond the classroom, and I strive to make myself available to students outside of class to address their questions and concerns. I also focus on creating an interactive and collaborative learning environment to foster critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

I am dedicated to providing quality education in the subjects of computer programming and software engineering. I have a strong background in these areas, with a Ph.D. in Signal Processing from Nanyang Technological University. I have previously taught courses such as Circuit Analysis, Signals and Systems, Digital Signal Processing, Computer Programming, and Software Engineering at institutions like Muhammad Ali Jinnah University, NED, and FAST.

I firmly believe that teaching is a continuous process, and I constantly strive to improve my teaching methods based on student feedback. I value the input of my students and use it to inform and enhance my teaching practices.

In order to create an inclusive learning environment, I make efforts to ensure that all students feel valued and supported. I promote an atmosphere of respect and open communication where students are encouraged to ask questions and engage in discussions. I aim to create a safe space where students feel comfortable expressing their thoughts and opinions.

I am also interested in exploring new techniques, activities, and types of learning to enhance student engagement and understanding. I am particularly fond of the top-down approach, which involves presenting the bigger picture and overall context of a subject before diving into the details. This approach allows students to develop a deeper understanding and encourages critical thinking.

In conclusion, my teaching philosophy revolves around creating an interactive and inclusive learning environment, fostering critical thinking skills, and empowering students to apply their knowledge to real-world situations. I am committed to continuous improvement and staying updated with new teaching techniques to facilitate effective student learning.
