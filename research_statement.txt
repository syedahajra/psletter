Research Statement:

I am S. M. Monir, a Ph.D. holder with a specialization in Signal Processing. My previous research primarily focused on image watermarking and steganography, where I developed novel methods for zero-watermarking and determined just-noticeable difference profiles for image hashing. These contributions have provided reliable authentication and copyright protection for color and grayscale images. However, my current position as the CEO of a software company concentrating on AI-based applications has sparked my interest in exploring the intersection of Software Engineering and AI.

In my current research, I am investigating the application of deep learning networks, specifically convolutional neural networks (CNNs). The significance of this research lies in the need to understand the performance of deep learning algorithms on problems beyond the scope of initial experiments. Additionally, adapting these algorithms to meet specific hardware requirements is essential. To address these challenges, I am focusing on the development of quantized models for edge processing units. By optimizing computational and memory requirements, these models can be efficiently deployed on edge devices, reducing the load on central servers and providing redundancy in case of device failure. The research outcomes have the potential to unlock new possibilities in distributed computing, particularly for computer vision systems utilizing multiple cameras in access control, quality control, and surveillance.

Looking forward, my future research will center on software engineering for AI-based applications. With the increasing prevalence of AI-powered software systems, there is a need to revitalize the traditional software development life cycle (SDLC) to cater to the unique requirements of AI. Inspired by DevOps and MLOps, I believe that agile methodologies can be adapted to AI applications. By incorporating agile principles into the SDLC of AI-based software, we can expedite development cycles, adapt to changing requirements, and optimize the integration of AI components. This research has the potential to revolutionize the SDLC and facilitate rapid development, enhanced software quality, and shorter time-to-market for AI-powered applications.

The importance and relevance of this research extend beyond software engineering. It can have a significant cross-disciplinary impact, influencing industries such as healthcare, finance, transportation, and entertainment, which heavily rely on AI technologies. This research aligns with my passion for innovation and my commitment to solving real-world challenges. I am excited about the potential outcomes of this research and the opportunity to contribute to the advancement of AI-based software engineering practices.

Overall, my research journey has evolved from image watermarking and steganography to the development of AI-based applications. Building upon the insights gained from my previous research, I am eager to explore the intersections of Software Engineering and AI to revolutionize the SDLC and facilitate the efficient deployment of AI-powered software systems. I am committed to making significant contributions, both academically and practically, and I am eager to collaborate with a diverse team of researchers and industry professionals to drive innovation and advancements in this exciting field.
